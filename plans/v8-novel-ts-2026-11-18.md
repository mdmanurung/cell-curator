## Why accuracy is low

- 33,283 of 82,785 blood cells (40%) have a truth term outside the corpus. They are neutrophil CL:0000775 (31,774), macrophage CL:0000235 (1,228), mature NK T cell CL:0000814 (183), hematopoietic stem cell CL:0000037 (57) and basophil CL:0000767 (41), counted from the truth file after scoring. The strict consensus abstains on 525 of them (1.6%). CellTypist and popV abstain on none, and SingleR on 135.
- On cells whose truth is a corpus term (17,269), strict consensus descendant accuracy is 0.931 at coverage 0.971. The tools score 0.846 (CellTypist), 0.914 (popV) and 0.909 (SingleR).
- On cells whose truth is a coarser parent of corpus terms (32,233), the consensus scores 0.879. The tools score 0.886, 0.844 and 0.854.

The low headline comes from cell types the corpus does not cover, which every method labels as some corpus type. This is an interpretation of the strata table, not a tested claim.

## Findings to carry forward

1. The skill can output labels outside the plan vocabulary. The package then refuses the import. This is a contract gap that the dev sets never hit.
2. No method abstains on out-of-corpus cell types, the consensus included. Whole-blood neutrophils are the largest such group.
3. The pass bars as written do not separate "wrong" from "outside the vocabulary". Any change to the bars or the method is a new protocol version, decided before any new held-out set is read.
